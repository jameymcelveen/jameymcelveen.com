I've been looking for work for several months now. I won't dress it up. It's been hard.

I've been building software for 30 years, and I've never seen a market like this one. You send a resume you're proud of and hear nothing. You get a call, feel hope, and then the role gets cut before the second interview. Some days it feels like shouting into a well.

My wife has been a rock through all of it. So has my faith. But I'd be lying if I said there weren't mornings when I sat down at the keyboard and wondered what the point was.

I know where I want to be. I spent 27 years building software for churches, and I want to work at a place where faith is part of the culture. Ministries. Christian schools. Companies that pray before a meeting and mean it. That's where I want to plant my feet for the long haul.

The trouble is, those jobs are hard to find. They're scattered across a hundred career pages. The big job boards don't have a filter for "people who share my faith."

So I did what I know how to do. I built one.

## Faith Wanted

[faithwanted.com](https://faithwanted.com) is a directory of Christian culture employers, from K-LOVE to Chick-fil-A, and the jobs they post.

A few things make it different:

- **Company first.** Every company is listed with the public reason it belongs there.
- **Human reviewed.** Nothing goes up until a person has looked at it. Right now that person is me.
- **The rules are public.** You can read exactly how a company gets listed.

It started with 85 companies and 20 open jobs. That's a start, not a finish.

## I need your help

I can't find them all by myself. If you know a company where faith is part of the culture, or you've seen a job at one, please send it my way. There's a short form at [faithwanted.com/submit](https://faithwanted.com/submit). It takes about a minute.

It doesn't have to be a tech job. It doesn't have to be a big company. If it would help a brother or sister find good work, I want to list it.

I built this because I needed it. My hope is that it helps somebody else who's in the same spot I'm in. If that's you, hang in there. You're not the only one.

Jamey
