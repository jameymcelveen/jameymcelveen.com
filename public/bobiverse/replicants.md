## Replicative Drift (the important lore note)

Every cohort throws one wildly divergent clone, and each generation lands a
little further from Bob-1. Far enough out, a "Bob" barely identifies as Bob at
all. That's the whole engine of the series: same seed, diverging instances,
no two run the same way once they're light-years and years apart. (Sound
familiar.)
